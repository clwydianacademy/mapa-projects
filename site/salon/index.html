Clwydian Academy training salon

Hair by our learners, checked by a supervisor.

The training salon is where Clwydian Academy learners work on real clients in St Asaph. You pay a learner price, you choose how experienced your learner is, and a supervisor checks the work before you leave.

Opening soon

We are still confirming the salon’s opening dates, opening times and prices, so online booking is not open yet. Email hello@clwydianacademy.co.uk and we will tell you when it is.

How it works

  1. Choose a service and a level

    Each service is offered by learners at different stages of their training. The earlier the stage, the lower the price.

  2. Allow the time

    A learner takes longer than a qualified stylist — that is the point of a training salon. We tell you the real length of your appointment before you book, not the length a salon would quote.

  3. An educator checks the work

    Every appointment starts with a consultation with the educator and ends with the educator checking the work.

  4. Colour needs an allergy alert test first

    For colour and lightening you need a free allergy alert test (patch test) with us at least 48 hours before your appointment. It is valid for 12 months, for the same product.

When the salon is open

The salon opens in term time only.
