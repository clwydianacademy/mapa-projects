What your hair adds

Colour prices start from short, fine, natural hair. Longer, thicker or previously coloured hair adds to the price, and we tell you the exact figure before you book.

Hair with box dye or bleach on it, and going three shades or more lighter, is Level 3 work and is not offered with a Level 2 learner.

Concession

20% off cutting and styling — not colour — on Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, for: Over 65, Students, NHS and care staff. Tell us when you book, and bring proof on the day.

Deposit

A deposit is paid by card when you book: £5 for cutting and styling, £10 for colour and technical services (never more than the price). It comes off your bill on the day. It is kept if you cancel with less than 48 hours’ notice or do not arrive.

Before colour

A free allergy alert test at least 48 hours before any colour. A free consultation before balayage and colour correction.

Under 16

Children under 12: the children’s cut. Ages 12 to 15: gents and children’s cuts, at those prices. A parent or guardian must be present. No permanent or quasi-permanent colour, bleach or relaxer under 16.
