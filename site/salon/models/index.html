Clwydian Academy training salon

Be a model for our learners

Learners need people to practise on and to be assessed on. Join the register and we will email you when a learner needs a model for something you are interested in.

Draft prices

These prices and times are a draft and have not been confirmed. They may change before the salon opens.

For an assessment, a cut or blow-dry is free. For colour you pay the cost of the product only, £10 to £15. A £5 deposit holds your place and is refunded when you attend. Joining the register commits you to nothing.
