Clwydian Academy training salon

Booking terms

Draft. These terms have not been approved yet and no booking can be made under them.

Who you are booking with

The training salon is run by Clwydian Academy CBC. Services are carried out by learners under supervision.

Time

Learners take longer than a qualified stylist. The length shown when you book is the time to allow.

Prices

The price you are shown when you book is the price you pay. Prices include VAT where it applies.

Deposit

A deposit is paid by card when you book: £5 for cutting and styling, £10 for colour and technical services (never more than the price). It comes off your bill on the day. It is kept if you cancel with less than 48 hours’ notice or do not arrive.

Changing or cancelling

Email hello@clwydianacademy.co.uk at least 48 hours before your appointment and we will move it or refund your deposit. With less notice, or if you do not arrive, the deposit is kept.

If we have to move or cancel

This is a training salon: a learner’s availability, or what a learner needs for an assessment, may mean we have to move your appointment. If we do, we will offer another time or refund your deposit in full.

Allergy alert tests and consultations

Colour and lightening services need an allergy alert test (patch test) with us at least 48 hours before the appointment and no more than 12 months before it, for the same product. Without one, the service cannot go ahead. Balayage and colour correction need a consultation first. Both are free.

Under 16

We do not carry out permanent or quasi-permanent colour, bleach or relaxer on anyone under 16. Other services for someone under 16 are limited to those on our price list, and a parent or guardian must consent and be present.

Your information

We keep your name and contact details to manage your booking. Consultation and allergy answers are health information: we keep them only with your consent, encrypted, and only supervising staff can read them. See our privacy notice.
