For salons · CPD for existing staff

Seven units. Fifty-four dates. One published price list.

Everything on the CPD calendar is a VTCT unit with an awarding-body registration and, where the unit demands it, an external paper. The credit banks toward a VTCT diploma. That is the difference between this and a day course.

The unit most salons start with

Skin Cancer Awareness

One day, £195, no prior qualification, no models, no exam. It is the cheapest way to see how we teach and the easiest day to cover on the rota. Two dates — 6 January and 28 July.

The one nobody else in the region can sell you

Relaxing & Smoothing (UHB205)

The only regulated route to relaxing competence in either VTCT family. There is no relaxing unit in the newer HBN suite at all. Eight Mondays from 11 January, £695.

The one that changes what a chair can charge

Colour & Lightening (UNHB374)

The only Level 3 unit on the calendar — 158 guided hours of regulated credit that carry across into the VTCT Skills diplomas. Ten Wednesdays from 13 January, £835.

Cover, honestly

One person, one day a week.

Perming, extensions and salon business are six days. Consultation is five. Relaxing is eight and colour is ten. Plan the rota around that and nothing else changes.
