The tariff

How every price is set

Every seat price on this page is one formula: £160.81 + £4.274 × guided learning hours, rounded to the nearest £5.

It is anchored on two published prices — UV21577 £195 @ 8 GLH and UHB205 £695 @ 125 GLH — and Hair Extensions carries a £50 supplement for brand-partner hair and kit.

Team rates

Book from one salon, gym or clinic and the whole booking is discounted:

  • 3–5 people: 10% off
  • 6–9 people: 15% off
  • 10 people (a whole cohort): 20% off

Academy closures

No cohort meets on these days; the dates above already skip them.

  • Friday 26 March 2027 — Good Friday
  • Monday 29 March 2027 — Easter Monday
  • Week commencing 5 April 2027 — Academy closed
  • Monday 3 May 2027 — May Day
  • Monday 31 May 2027 — Spring bank holiday
