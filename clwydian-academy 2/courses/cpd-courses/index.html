How CPD runs

Built around a working column.

Masterclasses run on consecutive Mondays — five to eight of them, or a single day for Skin Cancer Awareness — with theory handled by guided distance learning between sessions. Groups stay small, models are live, and every course closes with the commercial piece: what to charge, how to launch the service, and how to fill it.

Every 2027 cohort — dates, prices, levels and guided learning hours — is on the CPD calendar, together with the tariff the prices come from.

  • Designed and led by senior, education-qualified practitioners
  • Certificates of professional development for every course
  • Priority booking for salons developing whole teams — see our salon services
  • Progression advice into NVQ Level 3 where it fits your goals
