The formula

Seat price = £160.81 + £4.274 × guided learning hours, rounded to the nearest £5.

The fixed part covers enrolment, awarding-body registration, assessment and independent internal quality assurance — costs that fall on a one-day award as heavily as on a ten-week unit. The hourly part covers tutor time, the floor, products and models. It is anchored on UV21577 £195 @ 8 GLH and UHB205 £695 @ 125 GLH; Hair Extensions carries a £50 supplement for brand-partner hair and kit.

Team rates

  • 3–5 seats: 10% off
  • 6–9 seats: 15% off
  • A whole cohort of 10: 20% off

Counted by people from your salon across the whole term, not bookings on one date. One invoice, payable in instalments across the course.

Why we are not £195 for everything

Compared on price alone, the comparison is wrong.

Attendance courses locally run one day at around £175 to £335 and end in a certificate an insurer will accept. They have their place. They do not carry regulated credit. Every course here is a VTCT unit with independent quality assurance and, where the unit demands it, an external paper — and the credit banks toward a VTCT diploma.
