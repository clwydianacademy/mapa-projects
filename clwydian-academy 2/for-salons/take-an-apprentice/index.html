What the academy does

  • Delivers the Welsh Government apprenticeship framework in full — hairdressing or barbering — documented for employers and funders
  • Runs the structured academy blocks and the distance learning between them, at the fastest pace the framework allows
  • Assesses to VTCT NVQ standard with independent internal quality assurance, in a digital portfolio with unannounced workplace sampling
  • Walks both apprentice and employer through the Welsh apprenticeship funding paperwork, step by step
  • Gives the employer a dedicated point of contact, not a call centre

What the salon does

  • Employs the apprentice and pays the wage
  • Provides the salon floor, real clients and the workplace hours the framework requires
  • Releases them for the structured academy blocks
  • Allows unannounced workplace sampling as part of quality assurance
  • Signs the apprenticeship agreement, set up to Welsh Government requirements — hours, content and assessment documented from day one

What it costs the salon

The wage. Never the course.

Training is fully funded for eligible apprentices — you pay the wage, never the course. We check eligibility with you before anything is signed. All funding routes →

Where they go next

The graduate routes we support.

  • Employment — most stay where they trained; salons on our books hear first when a graduate is looking for a chair
  • Chair rental — the commercial resilience module covers pricing, rebooking and the business of a rented chair
  • A mobile business — fully supported, for the graduate who wants their own name on the van

Progression continues into Level 3 and the CPD masterclasses.

How to register interest.

Register your salon — with or without an apprentice in mind. We confirm the funded route, set up the agreement under the framework, and train at pace.
