The problem

It is not that your team won’t learn.

It is that teaching them costs you your best hour. In-salon training takes a senior stylist off the floor to coach a junior, and both columns stop earning. Send them to college instead and you lose them two days a week for two years. Neither of those is a plan; they are what happens when there is no third option.

What we actually do

One person, one day a week, back with a regulated unit.

We take one member of your team, one day a week, for between {{cpd:minDays}} and {{cpd:maxDays}} weeks depending on the unit, and we return them with a regulated VTCT unit. The rest of the week they are on your floor. The learning that does not need a model or a tutor is timetabled as distance learning, so the academy day is all hands-on.

Training is delivered by education-qualified senior practitioners with independent internal quality assurance, in a purpose-fitted St Asaph academy with professional-grade stations and COSHH-compliant ventilation.

Why two strands

Most salons close on Mondays. Some cannot.

So the same unit runs on both days across the term, and the commercial units run twice. A salon that misses a January start is not waiting until autumn.
