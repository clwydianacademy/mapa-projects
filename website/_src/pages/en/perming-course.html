The six Mondays

  • Week 1 — chemistry and precision. The science of structural change, elasticity and porosity testing, chemical selection, clean tension and placement
  • Week 2 — the three commercial winds. Standard wind for uniform body, brick wind for seamless blends, directional wind for modern movement and barbering trends
  • Week 3 — chemical dynamics + external exam. Neutralising, preventing and fixing fish-hooks and band marks, diffuser styling — then your official VTCT theory exam
  • Week 4 — live models and graduation. Practical sign-off under VTCT assessor supervision
