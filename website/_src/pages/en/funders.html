Our mission

Rebuild the route into a skilled trade — fast, bilingual, and owned by the community it serves.

Clwydian Academy CBC exists because the trade’s training pipeline collapsed: apprenticeship starts down around 70% in a decade, 92% of salons unable to take on an apprentice, and salons closing across Denbighshire, Flintshire and the border counties. We train apprentices who stay employed in their salons, take adults from no experience to NVQ Level 2 in six months rather than two years, and run CPD that makes a skill chargeable in a week — in Welsh and English, with interest-free instalments and no credit checks. We are an asset-locked Community Interest Company: every surplus is reinvested in training people from this community, and that lock is constitutional, not a promise.

What we are working towards

Medr’s new Welsh Apprenticeship Programme starts in August 2027, and its provider commissioning documents publish in September 2026 — the most important funding document of our first year. This plan is sequenced so the academy is accredited, staffed and evidenced when that round opens.

The advocacy we carry

Fair funding for this trade

Hairdressing is not on the Personal Learning Account priority list, so no academy can honestly offer PLA-funded places today. We are making the sector’s case to change that — and building the bilingual, hybrid delivery that funded adult places will need.

Governance

Built to be audited.

  • Constitutional asset lock on the CIC; surpluses reinvested in the mission
  • Independent director on the board and bank mandate; dual unrelated signatories — met before any funder asked
  • Complete legal and financial separation from the founders’ commercial interests: no grant income reaches any related company, and any service arrangement is written, at arm’s length, approved only by non-conflicted directors
  • Community purpose regulated by the CIC Regulator and disclosed annually in a public CIC34 report
  • VTCT Approved Centre application in progress (engagement opened June 2026); assessment and internal quality assurance lawfully separated from day one through a contracted independent IQA
