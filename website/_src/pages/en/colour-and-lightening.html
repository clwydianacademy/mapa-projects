What you will do

  • The three colouring and lightening techniques the unit requires — full-head colouring, re-growth colouring, and woven foil highlights and lowlights — on live clients, across three observed sessions
  • Consultation at Level 3 — the service plan, service history and informed consent, and the six diagnostic tests, before any colour service
  • Colour theory applied to real heads — the International Colour Code, depth and tone, neutralising unwanted tone, the percentage of white hair — and formulation for real client briefs
  • Colour correction principles — problems, causes and remedies — and reading service history and build-up before planning the route back
  • The product families — semi-, demi- and permanent colour, lighteners, toners, barrier cream and protectors — developer strengths and lift, and the health and safety protocol behind all fourteen steps of the service

Why this one is different

Every other masterclass we run is a Level 2 unit.

This is Level {{cpd:level:UNHB374}}, and that is not a marketing tier — it is {{cpd:glh:UNHB374}} guided learning hours of regulated credit. A stylist who takes this course is not holding a certificate of attendance at the end of it. They hold a regulated unit certificate, with the evidence in a portfolio that carries across into the VTCT Skills hairdressing and barbering diplomas if they go on to one.

It is also the unit that most changes what a chair can charge. Colour correction is the work clients cannot do at home and will not risk on an inexperienced hand, and it is the service most often turned away because nobody in the salon is confident enough to quote for it.

How it is assessed

Three things, and the written ones are real exams, not a formality: a portfolio built across the {{cpd:ndaysWord:UNHB374}} days, a unit paper, and an overarching paper. Both papers are set and marked by VTCT Skills. We will tell you well before the date what each one covers — nobody sits a paper here without having seen the shape of it first.

What you need to bring

A skin test booked and recorded before every client session, without exception. This is an awarding-body requirement and a safety one, and it is the single thing most likely to cost you a session if it slips. Sessions run to a colour schedule, not to walk-ins — we will send you the booking pattern once your seat is confirmed.

Where it leads

Credit that carries, not a certificate in a drawer.

The unit carries across into the VTCT Skills Level 2 diplomas in hairdressing and barbering for anyone who goes on to one; taken on its own it is a regulated Level {{cpd:level:UNHB374}} unit certificate. For a salon it is the difference between sending colour work away and quoting for it. For a stylist it is the start of the senior ladder — the technical services that command premium prices, and the confidence to price them.
