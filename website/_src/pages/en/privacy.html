Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: this website stores nothing about you, and we only ever hold what you choose to send us.

Who we are

Clwydian Academy CBC is a community benefit company, constituted as a Community Interest Company, based in St Asaph, Denbighshire, North Wales. For anything in this policy, contact {{site:email}}.

What this website collects

Nothing, by itself. This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies and runs no trackers. Fonts are served by Google Fonts, whose servers receive standard technical request data (such as your IP address) when the font files load; no other third-party services run on the site. Our web host (Vercel) may keep standard server logs, which we do not access for profiling.

When you contact us

When you send the enquiry form, it goes to our own server and we email you straight back to confirm we have it. Nothing passes through your own email app.

What we receive: your name and email address; your phone number, salon or business name if you choose to give them; which course or service you are asking about; your message; and whether you would like us to reply in Welsh or English.

What we do with it: we reply to your enquiry, and we keep you informed about the specific course or service you asked about. Nothing else. We do not sell your details, we do not share them for marketing, and we do not add you to a general mailing list on the strength of an enquiry.

Who else handles it: our website is hosted by Vercel, and the two emails — the one to us and the confirmation to you — are delivered by Resend. Both act on our instructions and neither uses your details for anything of their own. We use no analytics, advertising or tracking services on this site.

Why we are allowed to: you asked us a question and we are answering it. Where you are asking about training you might buy, we are taking steps at your request before entering a contract.

How long we keep it: we hold enquiries for 24 months from your last contact with us, then delete them. If you enrol, your learner record is kept separately and for longer, because an awarding body requires it — see below.

Spam protection: the form carries a hidden field and a timing check to stop automated submissions. Neither collects anything about you and neither is stored.

If you enrol

Learner records are a different matter from enquiries: VTCT and the funding body both require them, and they are kept for as long as those requirements say. We will state that period here plainly once the awarding body has confirmed it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you, and you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) if you believe we’ve handled your data improperly. Email us and we’ll act promptly.
