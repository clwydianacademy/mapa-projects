The formula

Seat price = {{cpd:fixed}} + {{cpd:perGlh}} × guided learning hours, rounded to the nearest {{cpd:roundTo}}.

The fixed part covers enrolment, awarding-body registration, assessment and independent internal quality assurance — costs that fall on a one-day award as heavily as on a ten-week unit. The hourly part covers tutor time, the floor, products and models. It is anchored on {{cpd:anchors}}; Hair Extensions carries a {{cpd:kit}} supplement for brand-partner hair and kit.

Why we are not {{cpd:minPrice}} for everything

Compared on price alone, the comparison is wrong.

Attendance courses locally run one day at around {{fee:marketDayMin}} to {{fee:marketDayMax}} and end in a certificate an insurer will accept. They have their place. They do not carry regulated credit. Every course here is a VTCT unit with independent quality assurance and, where the unit demands it, an external paper — and the credit banks toward a VTCT diploma.
